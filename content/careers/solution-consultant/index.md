## What you'll do

- Solution design, demos and PoCs
- Write proposals and SOWs
- Steer customer projects (e.g. large infrastructure / real-estate) and coordinate delivery
- Be the bridge between customer, product and tech
- Support closing on complex deals

## What you bring

- Deep AEC/BIM knowledge (IFC, ISO 19650, CDE) and Microsoft 365 / Power Platform (SharePoint, Power BI)
- Background e.g. at Dalux, Autodesk, Trimble, Nemetschek or Catenda
- Credible with customers and technically strong
- Calm, conscientious, mission-driven
- Own network a plus
- Not pure sales, not pure dev

## What we offer

- Key role in building the company
- Fix salary (project-de-risked start)
- Hybrid with on-site customer work
- Real ownership

## How to apply

Show us a solution you built or sold in an AEC context — and tell us why openBIM excites you.
