## What you'll do

- Qualify inbound leads (booking form, email)
- Run first calls and understand the real need
- Credibly answer ~80% of BIM/M365 questions yourself
- Log everything in Dynamics
- Hand deep/technical cases to our Solution Consultant
- Keep the pipeline warm
- In quiet phases, reactivate deals through your own industry network

## What you bring

- Solid AEC/BIM understanding (IFC, ISO 19650) and Microsoft 365 context
- Your own industry network you can reactivate (asset owners, construction, engineering, authorities)
- Consultative, credible, calm and conscientious
- Not a pure phone-SDR, not a pure developer
- Fluent English; German a strong plus

## What we offer

- A mission with substance
- Flat, remote-first team
- Fair base + commission (upside scales with the deals you win)
- Real ownership in building our go-to-market engine

## How to apply

No standard cover letter — tell us in 5 sentences: why this mission and what your AEC network looks like.
