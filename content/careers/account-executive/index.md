## What you'll do

- Take qualified opportunities from Inbound and the Solution Consultant
- Run follow-up meetings
- Build and manage pipeline
- Sell consultatively (with our Solution Consultant on the technical depth)
- Close
- In quiet phases, open doors through your own network

## What you bring

- Proven B2B closing in AEC/construction/engineering or the Microsoft ecosystem
- Enough BIM/IFC/ISO 19650 understanding to be credible
- Your own industry network
- Calm, conscientious, mission-driven, detail oriented

## What we offer

- Mission with substance
- Remote/hybrid
- Fair base + commission (upside scales with deals)
- Real ownership

## How to apply

No standard cover letter — in 5 sentences: why this mission, and your biggest AEC deal or network.
