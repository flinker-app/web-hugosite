Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find an answer? Check our docs or contact support

Is this an online IFC viewer if files stay on my device?

Yes. The viewer runs in your browser but processes files locally on your device by default. Your model data never leaves your computer unless you explicitly save to SharePoint or Teams.

Which IFC versions are supported?

We support IFC 2x3, IFC 4, and IFC 4x3 with accelerated rendering optimized for enterprise-scale datasets.

What's the difference between Community and Enterprise?

Community is free forever and includes the full product: view, filter, and analyze IFC models, save Excel and BCF to SharePoint or Teams, sync BCF to Microsoft Lists, and use IFC Copilot. Everything runs self-hosted in your own Microsoft 365 tenant. Enterprise is for organizations rolling out in production and adds priority support with SLA, guided deployment, volume and project-based licensing, framework agreements, and custom integrations.

How do I install the app in Teams or SharePoint?

Get it from Microsoft AppSource and add it to your Teams channel or SharePoint site. No admin approval needed for most organizations. See our setup guide for step-by-step instructions.

Can I embed the IFC Viewer in my platform?

Yes. Enterprise licensing is available for embedding the viewer in your CDE, digital twin, or custom application. See our developer docs or white-label options.
